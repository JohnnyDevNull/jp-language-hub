---
title: Swedish preterite
description: How Swedish forms and uses the simple past tense.
---

The Swedish preterite places an event or state in a past time frame. It is the
normal tense for completed past events, narratives, and descriptions of what
happened then.

## Core rule

Like all Swedish finite verb forms, the preterite is identical for every
subject:

- <span lang="sv">Jag arbetade.</span>
- <span lang="sv">Hon arbetade.</span>
- <span lang="sv">De arbetade.</span>

The form depends on the verb group:

| Pattern | Infinitive | Preterite | Supine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | <span lang="sv">tala</span> | <span lang="sv">talade</span> | <span lang="sv">talat</span> |
| Group 2 | <span lang="sv">stänga</span> | <span lang="sv">stängde</span> | <span lang="sv">stängt</span> |
| Group 2 | <span lang="sv">köpa</span> | <span lang="sv">köpte</span> | <span lang="sv">köpt</span> |
| Group 3 | <span lang="sv">bo</span> | <span lang="sv">bodde</span> | <span lang="sv">bott</span> |
| Irregular | <span lang="sv">skriva</span> | <span lang="sv">skrev</span> | <span lang="sv">skrivit</span> |

See [Swedish verb groups](../swedish-verb-groups/) for the basic patterns.

## When to use it

### A completed past event

<span lang="sv">Jag köpte boken i går.</span> — I bought the book yesterday.

### A past sequence

<span lang="sv">Hon vaknade, åt frukost och gick till jobbet.</span>

The three verbs move the story forward: she woke up, ate breakfast, and went to
work.

### A past state or habit

<span lang="sv">När jag bodde i Göteborg cyklade jag till jobbet.</span>

The preterite can describe both repeated actions and states in a finished past
period.

## Preterite or perfect?

Use the preterite when the event belongs to a clearly completed past frame:

<span lang="sv">Jag såg filmen förra veckan.</span>

Use the perfect when the present result or experience is central and the event
is not placed in a completed past frame:

<span lang="sv">Jag har sett filmen.</span>

This distinction is about perspective, not merely the presence of a time word.
An ongoing time period can still allow the perfect:

<span lang="sv">Jag har druckit två koppar kaffe idag.</span>

## Direct comparison

| Deutsch | English | Svenska |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="de">Ich kaufte gestern das Buch.</span> | <span lang="en">I bought the book yesterday.</span> | <span lang="sv">Jag köpte boken i går.</span> |
| <span lang="de">Als ich in Göteborg wohnte …</span> | <span lang="en">When I lived in Gothenburg …</span> | <span lang="sv">När jag bodde i Göteborg …</span> |

German often prefers the spoken perfect in contexts where Swedish naturally
uses the preterite. Choose the Swedish tense from its function, not by matching
the German surface form.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ <span lang="sv">Jag har köpt boken i går.</span>
  ✅ <span lang="sv">Jag köpte boken i går.</span>
- ❌ <span lang="sv">Hon köpade en bil.</span>
  ✅ <span lang="sv">Hon köpte en bil.</span>
- Do not form irregular verbs by attaching a regular ending. Learn their
  preterite and supine together.
