This section collects mistakes caused by transferring familiar patterns from
one language into another. Use the source-language sections to diagnose a
transfer pattern, or browse the cross-language categories for vocabulary and
translation traps.

## By source and target language

- [German to English](./de-to-en/) - German patterns that do not carry over to English.
- [German to Swedish](./de-to-sv/) - Swedish mistakes influenced by German.
- [English to Swedish](./en-to-sv/) - Swedish mistakes influenced by English.

## Across language pairs

- [False Friends](./false-friends/) - familiar-looking words with different meanings.
- [Direct Translation Errors](./direct-translation-errors/) - expressions that require a different construction or collocation.
