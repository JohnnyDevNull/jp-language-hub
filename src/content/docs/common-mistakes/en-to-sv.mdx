English is a useful bridge to Swedish vocabulary, but Swedish keeps several
word-order and agreement distinctions that English does not show.

## Word order after a fronted element

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Idag jag köper en bil."
	correct="Idag köper jag en bil."
	lang="sv"
>
	A fronted time expression occupies the first position, so the finite verb
	must come next. See [Swedish V2](../../grammar/sentence-structure/swedish/v2/).
</MistakeBox>

## Main clause order inside a subordinate clause

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="... eftersom jag kommer inte idag."
	correct="... eftersom jag inte kommer idag."
	lang="sv"
>
	In a typical Swedish subordinate clause, a sentence adverb such as `inte`
	precedes the finite verb. See
	[BIFF](../../grammar/sentence-structure/swedish/biff/).
</MistakeBox>

## Missing noun agreement

<MistakeBox incorrect="ett stor hus" correct="ett stort hus" lang="sv">
	The adjective agrees with an `ett` noun. See
	[adjective agreement](../../grammar/adjectives-adverbs/swedish/agreement/).
</MistakeBox>
