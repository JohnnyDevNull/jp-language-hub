German and English often use similar neutral sentence patterns, but they react
differently when another element is placed first.

## German inversion in an English statement

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Today am I buying a car."
	correct="Today I am buying a car."
	lang="en"
>
	German normally places the finite verb before the subject after a fronted time
	expression. English normally keeps the subject before the verb. See the
	[German-English-Swedish V2 comparison](../../grammar/sentence-structure/swedish/v2/).
</MistakeBox>
