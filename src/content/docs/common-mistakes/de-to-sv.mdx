German can be a useful bridge to Swedish, but similar-looking structures do not
always express the same distinction.

## Using a reflexive possessive for the wrong owner

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Anna såg Erik och hans hund. (if the dog belongs to Anna)"
	correct="Anna såg Erik och sin hund."
	lang="sv"
>
	Use `sin`, `sitt`, or `sina` when the possessive refers back to the subject of
	the same clause. See
	[reflexive possessives](../../grammar/pronouns-function-words/swedish/sin-sitt-sina/).
</MistakeBox>
