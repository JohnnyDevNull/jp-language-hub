Similar spelling does not guarantee the same meaning. Record false friends with
their intended meaning and an example rather than as isolated translation pairs.

## German *eventuell* and English *eventually*

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="I will eventually join the meeting. (if attendance is uncertain)"
	correct="I might join the meeting."
	lang="en"
>
	German `eventuell` means "possibly" or "maybe". English `eventually` means
	"in the end" or "after some time". See how the
	[vocabulary reference](../../vocabulary/) stores words with the context needed
	to use them correctly.
</MistakeBox>
