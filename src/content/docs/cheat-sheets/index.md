Cheat sheets provide a fast reminder after a topic has been learned. They stay
compact, link back to complete explanations, and remain usable on small screens.

## Initial targets

- Swedish en/ett and noun forms
- Swedish adjective agreement and comparison
- Swedish V2 and BIFF
- Swedish pronouns
- Swedish verb groups and verb forms
- German, English, and Swedish personal and possessive pronouns
- Tense comparison across the three languages

Detailed grammar pages remain the canonical source when a short overview is not
enough.
