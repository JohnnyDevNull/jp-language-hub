title: Vocabulary
description: Vocabulary organized by real-life domains and usage.
---

Vocabulary is organized by situations in which words are used, not as isolated
translation pairs. Entries will include the grammatical and usage information
needed to use a word correctly.

## Planned domains

- Basic vocabulary
- Home, food, and shopping
- Transport and travel
- People, relationships, and leisure
- Small talk, feelings, and opinions
- Society and everyday life in Sweden
- Work, meetings, and business communication

CEFR levels will be recorded as metadata where they are meaningful, but they
will not determine the primary navigation.
