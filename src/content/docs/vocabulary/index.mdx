Vocabulary is organized by situations in which words are used, not as isolated
translation pairs. Entries include the grammatical and usage information needed
to use a word correctly.

## Domains

- Basic vocabulary
- Home, food, and shopping
- Transport and travel
- People, relationships, and leisure
- Small talk, feelings, and opinions
- Society and everyday life in Sweden
- Work, meetings, and business communication

CEFR levels are metadata where they are meaningful, but they do not determine
the primary navigation.

## Store enough information to use a word

A Swedish noun is more useful as a compact set of forms than as a single
translation:

<VocabularyCard
	swedish="bil"

	forms={[
		{ label: 'Definite singular', value: 'bilen' },

Entries may also include example sentences, collocations, register, common
mistakes, synonyms, and controlled tags. Prepositions are recorded as part of
constructions such as `vänta på`, not as context-free translation pairs.
