<KeyTakeaway>
- <span lang="sv">tycker</span> states a personal judgement, <span lang="sv">tror</span> a belief about
  facts, <span lang="sv">vet</span> knowledge. German <span lang="de">glauben</span> and
  <span lang="de">finden</span> do not map one to one.
- Most opinion frames take an <span lang="sv">att</span>-clause, and <span lang="sv">att</span> is far less
  often dropped than English *that*.
- Negate the frame, not the content: <span lang="sv">Jag tror inte att han kommer.</span>
</KeyTakeaway>

These frames carry the sentence while you think about the rest of it. Learn them
as whole units, including the <span lang="sv">att</span> that follows.
For the grammar behind <span lang="sv">borde</span> and other modal choices, use
[Modal verbs](../../grammar/verb-patterns-voice/modal-verbs/). For reporting
someone else's statement instead of giving your own view, use
[Reported speech](../../grammar/mood-politeness-reports/reported-speech/).

## Stating an opinion

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">Jag tycker att …</span> | personal judgement, taste, evaluation | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Jag tror att …</span> | belief or guess about a fact | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Jag anser att …</span> | considered, argued position | formal |
| <span lang="sv">Enligt min mening …</span> | in my opinion | formal, written |
| <span lang="sv">Jag skulle säga att …</span> | hedged opinion | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">För mig är det …</span> | frames it as personal, not general | spoken |

## Marking uncertainty

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">Jag är inte säker på om …</span> | I am not sure whether | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Jag vet inte riktigt.</span> | I don't really know | spoken |
| <span lang="sv">Det beror på …</span> | it depends on | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Så vitt jag vet …</span> | as far as I know | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Jag har ingen aning.</span> | I have no idea | spoken, blunt |

## Making an assumption

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">Jag antar att …</span> | I assume | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Det verkar som om …</span> | it seems as if | neutral |
| <span lang="sv">Det låter som …</span> | that sounds like | spoken |
| <span lang="sv">Förmodligen …</span> / <span lang="sv">Troligen …</span> | probably | neutral to formal |
| <span lang="sv">Det borde vara …</span> | inference from evidence | neutral |

<span lang="sv">Förmodligen</span>, <span lang="sv">troligen</span> and <span lang="sv">så vitt jag vet</span> are
<span lang="sv">adverbial</span>. Put them first and the <span lang="sv">finit verb</span> still follows immediately —
V2 does not care that the phrase is three words long.

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Judgement: tycker">
	<p slot="de">Ich finde, dass der Film gut ist.</p>
	<p slot="en">I think the film is good.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag tycker att filmen är bra.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="Belief about a fact: tror">
	<p slot="de">Ich glaube, dass er kommt.</p>
	<p slot="en">I think he is coming.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag tror att han kommer.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

English *I think* covers both rows; Swedish forces the choice. German
<span lang="de">finden</span> lines up with <span lang="sv">tycker</span> and
<span lang="de">glauben</span> with <span lang="sv">tror</span>, which makes German the safer
bridge here.

<LanguageComparison title="Hedging">
	<p slot="de">Ich bin mir nicht sicher, ob das stimmt.</p>
	<p slot="en">I'm not sure if that's right.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag är inte säker på om det stämmer.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

The preposition is part of the pattern: <span lang="sv">säker på</span>, never
<span lang="sv">säker om</span>.
