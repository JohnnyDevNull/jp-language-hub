<KeyTakeaway>
- <Sv>tycker</Sv> states a personal judgement, <Sv>tror</Sv> a belief about
  facts, <Sv>vet</Sv> knowledge. German <span lang="de">glauben</span> and
  <span lang="de">finden</span> do not map one to one.
- Most opinion frames take an <Sv>att</Sv>-clause, and <Sv>att</Sv> is far less
  often dropped than English *that*.
- Negate the frame, not the content: <Sv>Jag tror inte att han kommer.</Sv>
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

These frames carry the sentence while you think about the rest of it. Learn them
as whole units, including the <Sv>att</Sv> that follows.

## Stating an opinion

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Jag tycker att …</Sv> | personal judgement, taste, evaluation | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag tror att …</Sv> | belief or guess about a fact | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag anser att …</Sv> | considered, argued position | formal |
| <Sv>Enligt min mening …</Sv> | in my opinion | formal, written |
| <Sv>Jag skulle säga att …</Sv> | hedged opinion | neutral |
| <Sv>För mig är det …</Sv> | frames it as personal, not general | spoken |

## Marking uncertainty

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Jag är inte säker på om …</Sv> | I am not sure whether | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag vet inte riktigt.</Sv> | I don't really know | spoken |
| <Sv>Det beror på …</Sv> | it depends on | neutral |
| <Sv>Så vitt jag vet …</Sv> | as far as I know | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag har ingen aning.</Sv> | I have no idea | spoken, blunt |

## Making an assumption

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Jag antar att …</Sv> | I assume | neutral |
| <Sv>Det verkar som om …</Sv> | it seems as if | neutral |
| <Sv>Det låter som …</Sv> | that sounds like | spoken |
| <Sv>Förmodligen …</Sv> / <Sv>Troligen …</Sv> | probably | neutral to formal |
| <Sv>Det borde vara …</Sv> | inference from evidence | neutral |

<Sv>Förmodligen</Sv>, <Sv>troligen</Sv> and <Sv>så vitt jag vet</Sv> are
adverbials. Put them first and the finite verb still follows immediately —
V2 does not care that the phrase is three words long.

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Judgement: tycker">
	<p slot="de">Ich finde, dass der Film gut ist.</p>
	<p slot="en">I think the film is good.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag tycker att filmen är bra.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="Belief about a fact: tror">
	<p slot="de">Ich glaube, dass er kommt.</p>
	<p slot="en">I think he is coming.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag tror att han kommer.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

English *I think* covers both rows; Swedish forces the choice. German
<span lang="de">finden</span> lines up with <Sv>tycker</Sv> and
<span lang="de">glauben</span> with <Sv>tror</Sv>, which makes German the safer
bridge here.

<LanguageComparison title="Hedging">
	<p slot="de">Ich bin mir nicht sicher, ob das stimmt.</p>
	<p slot="en">I'm not sure if that's right.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag är inte säker på om det stämmer.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

The preposition is part of the pattern: <Sv>säker på</Sv>, never
<Sv>säker om</Sv>.
