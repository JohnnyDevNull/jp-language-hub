Shopping Swedish is mostly a set of ready-made moves: find the item, check the
price or size, choose, and pay. Learn the short answers too, because cashiers
often ask without a full sentence.

## Finding and asking

| Pattern | Use it when | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Jag letar efter …</Sv> | you are looking for something | neutral |
| <Sv>Har ni …?</Sv> | asking whether the shop has it | neutral |
| <Sv>Var finns …?</Sv> | asking where something is | neutral |
| <Sv>Finns den i en annan storlek?</Sv> | asking for another size | neutral |
| <Sv>Finns den i en annan färg?</Sv> | asking for another color | neutral |
| <Sv>Kan jag prova den?</Sv> | asking to try it on | neutral |

Use <Sv>den</Sv> for an <Sv>en</Sv>-word item and <Sv>det</Sv> for an
<Sv>ett</Sv>-word item. If you do not know the gender yet, point and use
<Sv>den här</Sv> for "this one" in everyday speech.

## Choosing and paying

| Pattern | Use it when | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Hur mycket kostar den?</Sv> | asking the price of one item | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag tar den här.</Sv> | choosing this one | spoken |
| <Sv>Det blir bra.</Sv> | that will be fine | spoken |
| <Sv>Kan jag betala med kort?</Sv> | asking to pay by card | neutral |
| <Sv>Jag betalar med kort.</Sv> | stating how you pay | neutral |
| <Sv>Kan jag få kvittot?</Sv> | asking for the receipt | neutral |

## Checkout replies

| If they ask | Reply |
| --- | --- |
| <Sv>Kvitto?</Sv> | <Sv>Ja tack.</Sv> / <Sv>Nej tack.</Sv> |
| <Sv>Påse?</Sv> | <Sv>Ja, en påse tack.</Sv> |
| <Sv>Är det bra så?</Sv> | <Sv>Ja, det är bra.</Sv> |
| <Sv>Medlemskap?</Sv> | <Sv>Nej, jag är inte medlem.</Sv> |

## Tiny checkout

<p lang="sv">
	A: Hej! Behöver du hjälp?
	<br />
	B: Ja, jag letar efter en laddare.
	<br />
	A: De finns där borta.
	<br />
	B: Tack. Jag tar den här. Kan jag betala med kort?
</p>

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Looking for something">
	<p slot="de">Ich suche ein Ladegerät.</p>
	<p slot="en">I'm looking for a charger.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag letar efter en laddare.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

The Swedish pattern is <Sv>leta efter</Sv>, not a direct copy of German
<span lang="de">suchen</span> or English "look for" word by word.

<LanguageComparison title="Choosing this one">
	<p slot="de">Ich nehme das hier.</p>
	<p slot="en">I'll take this one.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag tar den här.</p>
</LanguageComparison>
