<KeyTakeaway>
- <Sv>Eftersom</Sv> is the neutral way to open a full sentence with "because";
  <Sv>därför att</Sv> is marked there, but normal after a main clause and as a
  short answer fragment.
- <Sv>därför</Sv> is the *consequence* adverb ("therefore") and takes the finite
  verb straight after it: <Sv>Därför stannar jag hemma.</Sv>
- Reason clauses are subordinate clauses, so <Sv>inte</Sv> goes before the
  finite verb inside them.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

The pair <Sv>därför</Sv> and <Sv>därför att</Sv> is the single most useful
distinction on this page, and the one German speakers get wrong most often.
For the broader connector system, use
[Connectors](../../grammar/prepositions-function-words/connectors/); this page
keeps the ready-to-use reason and example frames.

## Giving a reason

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>… eftersom …</Sv> | because; neutral inside or before the sentence | neutral |
| <Sv>… därför att …</Sv> | because; answers *why*, common after a main clause and as a fragment | neutral, marked before a full main clause |
| <Sv>… för …</Sv> | because, joining two main clauses | spoken |
| <Sv>Det beror på att …</Sv> | that is because | neutral |
| <Sv>Anledningen är att …</Sv> | the reason is that | neutral to formal |
| <Sv>på grund av …</Sv> + noun | because of | neutral |
| <Sv>tack vare …</Sv> + noun | thanks to; positive reason only | neutral |

## Explaining a point

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Det betyder att …</Sv> | that means that | neutral |
| <Sv>Med andra ord …</Sv> | in other words | neutral |
| <Sv>Vad jag menar är att …</Sv> | reformulating your own point | neutral |
| <Sv>alltså</Sv> | so, that is; also fills a pause | spoken |

## Giving an example and comparing

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>till exempel</Sv> (<Sv>t.ex.</Sv>) | for example | neutral |
| <Sv>Ta … som exempel.</Sv> | take X as an example | neutral |
| <Sv>jämfört med …</Sv> | compared with | neutral |
| <Sv>till skillnad från …</Sv> | unlike | neutral |
| <Sv>precis som …</Sv> | just like | neutral |
| <Sv>… medan …</Sv> | whereas, contrasting two facts | neutral |
| <Sv>å ena sidan … å andra sidan …</Sv> | on the one hand … on the other | formal |

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Reason clause in second position">
	<p slot="de">Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil es regnet.</p>
	<p slot="en">I'm staying home because it's raining.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Jag stannar hemma eftersom det regnar.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="Reason clause first">
	<p slot="de">Weil es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause.</p>
	<p slot="en">Because it's raining, I'm staying home.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Eftersom det regnar stannar jag hemma.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

The fronted clause is one constituent, so the finite verb <Sv>stannar</Sv>
follows it directly and the subject comes after the verb. <Sv>Eftersom</Sv> is
the neutral choice here. <Sv>Därför att det regnar stannar jag hemma</Sv> is
possible, but it sounds marked before a full main clause; use
<Sv>därför att</Sv> freely after a main clause or as a short answer:
<Sv>Varför stannar du hemma? Därför att det regnar.</Sv>

<LanguageComparison title="Consequence: därför">
	<p slot="de">Es regnet, deshalb bleibe ich zu Hause.</p>
	<p slot="en">It's raining, so I'm staying home.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Det regnar, därför stannar jag hemma.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="Contrasting two languages">
	<p slot="de">Im Gegensatz zum Deutschen hat Schwedisch hier keine Dativform.</p>
	<p slot="en">Unlike German, Swedish uses no dative form here.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Till skillnad från tyska använder svenska ingen dativform här.</p>
</LanguageComparison>
