<KeyTakeaway>
- Swedish keeps a conversation alive with very short questions:
  <Sv>Hur så?</Sv>, <Sv>Och sen då?</Sv>, <Sv>Hur menar du?</Sv>
- <Sv>Förresten</Sv> and <Sv>apropå</Sv> are the two standard topic-shift
  openers; <Sv>apropå</Sv> links to what was just said, <Sv>förresten</Sv> does
  not have to.
- An interruption is prefaced, not just started:
  <Sv>Förlåt att jag avbryter, men …</Sv>
</KeyTakeaway>

These are the phrases that make a conversation feel two-sided. None of them
carries content, which is exactly why they can be learned as fixed units.

## Asking a follow-up question

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Hur menar du?</Sv> | what do you mean | neutral |
| <Sv>Hur så?</Sv> | why is that, how come | spoken |
| <Sv>Och sen då?</Sv> | and then what | spoken |
| <Sv>Vad hände sen?</Sv> | what happened next | spoken |
| <Sv>Berätta mer!</Sv> | tell me more | spoken |
| <Sv>Menar du att …?</Sv> | checking your understanding | neutral |
| <Sv>Kan du utveckla det?</Sv> | can you elaborate | neutral to formal |

## Continuing and changing topic

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Just det, och …</Sv> | agree, then add | spoken |
| <Sv>Som sagt …</Sv> | as I said, returning to your point | neutral |
| <Sv>Apropå det …</Sv> | speaking of that | neutral |
| <Sv>Förresten …</Sv> | by the way | spoken |
| <Sv>En annan sak …</Sv> | another thing | spoken |
| <Sv>För att byta ämne …</Sv> | to change the subject | neutral |

## Interrupting politely

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Förlåt att jag avbryter, men …</Sv> | sorry to interrupt, but | neutral |
| <Sv>Ursäkta, men …</Sv> | excuse me, but | neutral |
| <Sv>Får jag säga en sak?</Sv> | may I say one thing | neutral |

## Summarizing

| Pattern | What it does | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Sv>Kort sagt …</Sv> | in short | neutral |
| <Sv>Sammanfattningsvis …</Sv> | in summary | formal, written |
| <Sv>Så det du säger är att …</Sv> | so what you're saying is | neutral |
| <Sv>Om jag förstår dig rätt …</Sv> | if I understand you correctly | neutral |
| <Sv>Med andra ord …</Sv> | in other words | neutral |

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="By the way">
	<p slot="de">Übrigens, ich habe Anna getroffen.</p>
	<p slot="en">By the way, I met Anna.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Förresten, jag träffade Anna.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="Prefacing an interruption">
	<p slot="de">Entschuldige, dass ich unterbreche, aber …</p>
	<p slot="en">Sorry to interrupt, but …</p>
	<p slot="sv">Förlåt att jag avbryter, men …</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<Sv>Förlåt</Sv> apologizes for something you are doing; <Sv>ursäkta</Sv> asks
for attention. Both work here, and the <Sv>att</Sv>-clause is obligatory in the
first pattern.

<LanguageComparison title="Playing a point back">
	<p slot="de">Du sagst also, dass es zu teuer ist.</p>
	<p slot="en">So what you're saying is that it's too expensive.</p>
	<p slot="sv">Så det du säger är att det är för dyrt.</p>
</LanguageComparison>
