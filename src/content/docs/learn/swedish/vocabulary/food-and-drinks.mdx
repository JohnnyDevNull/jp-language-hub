<KeyTakeaway>
- Learn food nouns with article and plural: <Sv>en bulle, bullar</Sv>.
- Use <Sv>jag skulle vilja ha</Sv> as the polite default when ordering.
- Small modifiers do a lot of work: <Sv>med</Sv>, <Sv>utan</Sv>,
  <Sv>lite</Sv>, <Sv>en till</Sv>.
</KeyTakeaway>

This page gives you enough food vocabulary to order something simple, read a
basic menu, and adjust what you ask for.

## Core words

| Use | Swedish | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| drink | <Sv>en kaffe, kaffe</Sv> | coffee |
| drink | <Sv>ett te, teer</Sv> | tea |
| drink | <Sv>ett vatten, vatten</Sv> | water |
| drink | <Sv>en mjölk</Sv> | milk |
| food | <Sv>en bulle, bullar</Sv> | bun |
| food | <Sv>en smörgås, smörgåsar</Sv> | sandwich |
| food | <Sv>en sallad, sallader</Sv> | salad |
| food | <Sv>en soppa, soppor</Sv> | soup |
| sweet | <Sv>en kaka, kakor</Sv> | cookie or cake |
| sweet | <Sv>en glass, glassar</Sv> | ice cream |

Mass nouns such as <Sv>kaffe</Sv>, <Sv>te</Sv>, and <Sv>vatten</Sv> often work
without a plural in everyday ordering. Use the article when you mean one item:
<Sv>en kaffe</Sv>, <Sv>ett vatten</Sv>.

## Ordering frames

| Need | Swedish frame |
| --- | --- |
| order politely | <Sv>Jag skulle vilja ha en kaffe.</Sv> |
| ask directly | <Sv>Kan jag få en smörgås?</Sv> |
| add something | <Sv>med mjölk</Sv>, <Sv>med ost</Sv> |
| remove something | <Sv>utan socker</Sv>, <Sv>utan lök</Sv> |
| ask for one more | <Sv>en till, tack</Sv> |
| ask what exists | <Sv>Finns det något vegetariskt?</Sv> |

| Swedish | English |

## Tiny dialogue

<p lang="sv">A: Hej! Vad vill du ha?</p>
<p lang="sv">B: Jag skulle vilja ha en kaffe och en bulle.</p>
<p lang="sv">A: Vill du ha mjölk?</p>
<p lang="sv">B: Ja tack, lite mjölk.</p>
