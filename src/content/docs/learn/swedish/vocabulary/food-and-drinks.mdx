This page gives you enough food vocabulary to order something simple, read a
basic menu, and adjust what you ask for.

## Core words

| Use | Swedish | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| drink | <span lang="sv">en kaffe, kaffe</span> | coffee |
| drink | <span lang="sv">ett te, teer</span> | tea |
| drink | <span lang="sv">ett vatten, vatten</span> | water |
| drink | <span lang="sv">en mjölk</span> | milk |
| food | <span lang="sv">en bulle, bullar</span> | bun |
| food | <span lang="sv">en smörgås, smörgåsar</span> | sandwich |
| food | <span lang="sv">en sallad, sallader</span> | salad |
| food | <span lang="sv">en soppa, soppor</span> | soup |
| sweet | <span lang="sv">en kaka, kakor</span> | cookie or cake |
| sweet | <span lang="sv">en glass, glassar</span> | ice cream |

Mass nouns such as <span lang="sv">kaffe</span>, <span lang="sv">te</span>, and <span lang="sv">vatten</span> often work
without a plural in everyday ordering. Use the article when you mean one item:
<span lang="sv">en kaffe</span>, <span lang="sv">ett vatten</span>.

## Ordering frames

| Need | Swedish frame |
| --- | --- |
| order politely | <span lang="sv">Jag skulle vilja ha en kaffe.</span> |
| ask directly | <span lang="sv">Kan jag få en smörgås?</span> |
| add something | <span lang="sv">med mjölk</span>, <span lang="sv">med ost</span> |
| remove something | <span lang="sv">utan socker</span>, <span lang="sv">utan lök</span> |
| ask for one more | <span lang="sv">en till, tack</span> |
| ask what exists | <span lang="sv">Finns det något vegetariskt?</span> |

| --- | --- |

## Tiny dialogue

<p lang="sv">A: Hej! Vad vill du ha?</p>
<p lang="sv">B: Jag skulle vilja ha en kaffe och en bulle.</p>
<p lang="sv">A: Vill du ha mjölk?</p>
<p lang="sv">B: Ja tack, lite mjölk.</p>
