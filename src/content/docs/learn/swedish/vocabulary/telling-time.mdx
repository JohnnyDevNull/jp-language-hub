Swedish clock time uses a small set of recurring words:
<span lang="sv">klockan</span>, <span lang="sv">över</span>,
<span lang="sv">i</span>, <span lang="sv">halv</span>, and
<span lang="sv">kvart</span>.

## Asking and answering

| Svenska | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">Vad är klockan?</span> | <span lang="de">Wie spät ist es?</span> | <span lang="en">What time is it?</span> |
| <span lang="sv">Klockan är nio.</span> | <span lang="de">Es ist neun Uhr.</span> | <span lang="en">It is nine o'clock.</span> |

Literally, <span lang="sv">Klockan är nio</span> is “the clock is nine”.

## Minutes after and before

Use <span lang="sv">över</span> for minutes after the hour:

<p lang="sv">Klockan är sju över två.</p>

Use <span lang="sv">i</span> for minutes before the next hour:

<p lang="sv">Klockan är fem i sex.</p>

| Svenska | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">sju över två</span> | <span lang="de">sieben nach zwei</span> | <span lang="en">seven past two</span> |
| <span lang="sv">fem i sex</span> | <span lang="de">fünf vor sechs</span> | <span lang="en">five to six</span> |

## Half hours

Swedish <span lang="sv">halv</span> works like German
<span lang="de">halb</span>: it points to the upcoming hour.

| Svenska | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">halv nio</span> | <span lang="de">halb neun</span> | <span lang="en">half past eight</span> |

You can also place minutes before or after the half-hour point:

| Svenska | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">tio i halv nio</span> | <span lang="de">zehn vor halb neun</span> | <span lang="en">twenty past eight</span> |
| <span lang="sv">tio över halv nio</span> | <span lang="de">zehn nach halb neun</span> | <span lang="en">twenty to nine</span> |

## Quarter hours

| Svenska | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="sv">kvart över fyra</span> | <span lang="de">Viertel nach vier</span> | <span lang="en">quarter past four</span> |
| <span lang="sv">kvart i sju</span> | <span lang="de">Viertel vor sieben</span> | <span lang="en">quarter to seven</span> |

## Quick pattern

| Meaning | Swedish pattern |
| --- | --- |
| exact hour | <span lang="sv">Klockan är nio.</span> |
| after the hour | <span lang="sv">X över Y</span> |
| before the next hour | <span lang="sv">X i Y</span> |
| half to the next hour | <span lang="sv">halv Y</span> |
| quarter after | <span lang="sv">kvart över Y</span> |
| quarter before | <span lang="sv">kvart i Y</span> |

<MistakeBox title="Common mistakes">
	- Do not read <span lang="sv">halv nio</span> as nine-thirty. It means
	  half way to nine: eight-thirty.
	- Do not translate English “past” and “to” through a general place rule.
	  Clock time uses its own small pattern.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
For German speakers, <span lang="sv">halv nio</span> behaves like
<span lang="de">halb neun</span>. For English speakers, translate it mentally
as “half past eight”.
</MemoryTip>
