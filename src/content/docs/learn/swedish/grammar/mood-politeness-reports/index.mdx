This shelf covers two related B-level tasks: presenting situations as more or
less real, and reporting what somebody said or asked.

## Choose by

| If you need to… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| express a real or open condition | [Conditionals](./conditionals/#real-and-open-conditions) |
| express an imagined present or future | [Conditionals](./conditionals/#present-and-future-hypothetical) |
| express a practical past counterfactual | [Conditionals](./conditionals/#past-counterfactual) |
| report a statement, yes/no question, or wh-question | [Reported speech](./reported-speech/) |
| grab a ready-made condition or consequence phrase | [Conditions and consequences](../../sentence-patterns/conditions-and-consequences/) |
| soften an everyday request | [Polite requests](../../sentence-patterns/polite-requests/) |

## Learn in this order

1. [Conditionals](./conditionals/) starts with real conditions and then adds
   hypothetical distance.
2. [Conditions and consequences](../../sentence-patterns/conditions-and-consequences/)
   turns those choices into reusable conversational frames.
3. [Reported speech](./reported-speech/) adds statement and question reports at
   B2.
