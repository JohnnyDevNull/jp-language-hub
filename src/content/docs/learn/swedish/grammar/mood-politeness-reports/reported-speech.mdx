<KeyTakeaway>
- Report a statement with <Sv>att</Sv>, a yes/no question with <Sv>om</Sv>, and
  a wh-question with its question word.
- Reported questions use subordinate-clause order, not direct-question
  inversion.
- Change pronouns and time or place words when the reporting viewpoint changes;
  Swedish tense does not require automatic English-style backshift.
</KeyTakeaway>

Reported speech presents the content of somebody's words without repeating the
original sentence as a quotation.

## Use this now

| Original message | Reporting link | Report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| statement | <Sv>att</Sv> | <Sv>Hon säger att hon kommer.</Sv> |
| yes/no question | <Sv>om</Sv> | <Sv>Han frågar om jag kommer.</Sv> |
| wh-question | same question word | <Sv>Hon frågar när tåget går.</Sv> |

## Statements with <Sv>att</Sv>

Use a reporting verb such as <Sv>säga</Sv>, <Sv>berätta</Sv>, or
<Sv>förklara</Sv> followed by an <Sv>att</Sv> clause:

<Sv>Lisa säger: “Jag är trött.”</Sv> →
<Sv>Lisa säger att hon är trött.</Sv>

## Reported questions

A yes/no question becomes an <Sv>om</Sv> clause. A wh-question keeps its
question word. Both use subordinate-clause order:

<SentenceSchema
	caption="Word order in reported questions"
	columns={['Link', 'Subject', 'Sentence adverb', 'Finite verb', 'Rest']}
	rows={[
		{ label: 'Yes/no report', lang: 'sv', cells: ['om', 'du', 'inte', 'kan', 'komma'] },
		{ label: 'Wh-report', lang: 'sv', cells: ['varför', 'hon', '', 'gick', 'hem'] },
	]}
/>

- <Sv>Han frågar om du inte kan komma.</Sv>
- <Sv>Jag undrar varför hon gick hem.</Sv>

Do not keep direct-question inversion: <Sv>varför hon gick</Sv>, not
<Sv>varför gick hon</Sv> inside the report.

## Viewpoint changes

Adjust forms only when the reporting situation requires it:

- person: <Sv>jag</Sv> may become <Sv>hon</Sv> or <Sv>han</Sv>
- possession: <Sv>min</Sv> may become <Sv>hennes</Sv>, <Sv>hans</Sv>, or a
  reflexive possessive in the relevant clause
- time: <Sv>i dag</Sv> may become <Sv>den dagen</Sv>
- place: <Sv>här</Sv> may become <Sv>där</Sv>

If the original viewpoint is still shared, a change may be unnecessary.

## Tense behavior

Swedish often keeps a tense that is still true or still relevant:
<Sv>Hon sa att hon bor i Malmö.</Sv> can report a current fact. A past form is
also natural when the report belongs to a past frame or the situation no longer
holds: <Sv>Hon sa att hon bodde i Malmö.</Sv>

Do not apply a mechanical English backshift rule. Choose the tense from the
speaker's current time perspective and the intended meaning.

<MistakeBox title="Common mistakes">
	- ❌ <Sv>Han frågar att jag kommer.</Sv>
	  ✅ <Sv>Han frågar om jag kommer.</Sv>
	- ❌ <Sv>Hon frågar var bor du.</Sv>
	  ✅ <Sv>Hon frågar var du bor.</Sv>
	- Do not change every present to a past merely because the reporting verb is
	  past.
</MistakeBox>
