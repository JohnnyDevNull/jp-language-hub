<KeyTakeaway>
- Use <span lang="sv">om</span> + present for a real or open condition; the consequence can
  use the present, an imperative, or a suitable future expression.
- Use preterite with <span lang="sv">skulle</span> for an imagined present or future.
- Use pluperfect with <span lang="sv">skulle ha</span> + supine for a past situation that did
  not happen.
</KeyTakeaway>

A conditional connects a condition with its consequence. The verb forms show
whether the speaker treats the condition as open, imagined, or contrary to a
known past.

## Use this now

| Meaning | Condition | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| real or open | <span lang="sv">om</span> + present | present, imperative, or future expression |
| imagined present/future | <span lang="sv">om</span> + preterite | <span lang="sv">skulle</span> + infinitive |
| unreal past | <span lang="sv">om</span> + pluperfect | <span lang="sv">skulle ha</span> + supine |

## Real and open conditions

Use present after <span lang="sv">om</span> when the condition is genuinely possible:

- <span lang="sv">Om det regnar, stannar vi hemma.</span>
- <span lang="sv">Om du har tid, ring mig.</span>
- <span lang="sv">Om tåget är sent, kommer jag att skriva.</span>

Do not add a future auxiliary automatically inside the <span lang="sv">om</span> clause.

## Present and future hypothetical

Use a preterite form in the condition and <span lang="sv">skulle</span> + infinitive in the
consequence when you imagine a less real present or future:

<span lang="sv">Om jag hade mer tid, skulle jag läsa mer.</span>

The preterite here signals distance from reality, not simply past time.

## Past counterfactual

For a past condition that did not happen, use pluperfect in the condition and
<span lang="sv">skulle ha</span> + supine in the consequence:

<span lang="sv">Om jag hade vetat det, skulle jag ha ringt.</span>

This practical B2 pattern is enough for most everyday counterfactuals.

## Alternatives to <span lang="sv">om</span>

<span lang="sv">Om inte</span> is the clearest general equivalent of *if not* and often of
*unless*: <span lang="sv">Jag går inte om du inte följer med.</span> Swedish also uses
expressions such as <span lang="sv">såvida inte</span> in more formal or restrictive
contexts. Choose the phrase that matches the intended condition rather than
assuming English *unless* has only one fixed Swedish form.

<MistakeBox title="Common mistakes">
	- Do not read every preterite in an <span lang="sv">om</span> clause as past time; it can
	  mark hypothetical distance.
	- <span lang="sv">Om jag hade mer tid, skulle jag läsa mer</span> is the neutral default
	  for an imagined present. <span lang="sv">Om jag skulle ha mer tid …</span> is also
	  grammatical, but presents the condition more tentatively or as a marked
	  possibility; do not label it as an error.
	- Keep the normal subordinate-clause order after <span lang="sv">om</span>; see
	  [Subordinate clauses](../../sentence-structure/subordinate-clauses/).
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Open: present. Imagined now or later: preterite + <span lang="sv">skulle</span>. Unreal past:
pluperfect + <span lang="sv">skulle ha</span>.
</MemoryTip>
