<KeyTakeaway>
- Use <Sv>om</Sv> + present for a real or open condition; the consequence can
  use the present, an imperative, or a suitable future expression.
- Use preterite with <Sv>skulle</Sv> for an imagined present or future.
- Use pluperfect with <Sv>skulle ha</Sv> + supine for a past situation that did
  not happen.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

A conditional connects a condition with its consequence. The verb forms show
whether the speaker treats the condition as open, imagined, or contrary to a
known past.

## Use this now

| Meaning | Condition | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| real or open | <Sv>om</Sv> + present | present, imperative, or future expression |
| imagined present/future | <Sv>om</Sv> + preterite | <Sv>skulle</Sv> + infinitive |
| unreal past | <Sv>om</Sv> + pluperfect | <Sv>skulle ha</Sv> + supine |

## Real and open conditions

Use present after <Sv>om</Sv> when the condition is genuinely possible:

- <Sv>Om det regnar, stannar vi hemma.</Sv>
- <Sv>Om du har tid, ring mig.</Sv>
- <Sv>Om tåget är sent, kommer jag att skriva.</Sv>

Do not add a future auxiliary automatically inside the <Sv>om</Sv> clause.

## Present and future hypothetical

Use a preterite form in the condition and <Sv>skulle</Sv> + infinitive in the
consequence when you imagine a less real present or future:

<Sv>Om jag hade mer tid, skulle jag läsa mer.</Sv>

The preterite here signals distance from reality, not simply past time.

## Past counterfactual

For a past condition that did not happen, use pluperfect in the condition and
<Sv>skulle ha</Sv> + supine in the consequence:

<Sv>Om jag hade vetat det, skulle jag ha ringt.</Sv>

This practical B2 pattern is enough for most everyday counterfactuals.

## Alternatives to <Sv>om</Sv>

<Sv>Om inte</Sv> is the clearest general equivalent of *if not* and often of
*unless*: <Sv>Jag går inte om du inte följer med.</Sv> Swedish also uses
expressions such as <Sv>såvida inte</Sv> in more formal or restrictive
contexts. Choose the phrase that matches the intended condition rather than
assuming English *unless* has only one fixed Swedish form.

<MistakeBox title="Common mistakes">
	- Do not read every preterite in an <Sv>om</Sv> clause as past time; it can
	  mark hypothetical distance.
	- <Sv>Om jag hade mer tid, skulle jag läsa mer</Sv> is the neutral default
	  for an imagined present. <Sv>Om jag skulle ha mer tid …</Sv> is also
	  grammatical, but presents the condition more tentatively or as a marked
	  possibility; do not label it as an error.
	- Keep the normal subordinate-clause order after <Sv>om</Sv>; see
	  [Subordinate clauses](../../sentence-structure/subordinate-clauses/).
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Open: present. Imagined now or later: preterite + <Sv>skulle</Sv>. Unreal past:
pluperfect + <Sv>skulle ha</Sv>.
</MemoryTip>
