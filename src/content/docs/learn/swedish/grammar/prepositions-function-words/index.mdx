## Learn in this order

1. [Prepositions](./prepositions/) gives the everyday place, direction, time,
   and fixed-phrase lookup.
2. [*där-* and *här-* adverbs](./dar-har-adverbs/) adds compressed forms and
   the practical <Sv>därför</Sv>/<Sv>därför att</Sv> contrast.
3. [Connectors](./connectors/) chooses the joining word for addition,
   contrast, alternatives, reasons, results, conditions, and examples.

## Watch for

- A preposition belongs to a noun or fixed verb expression; a particle belongs
  to the verb and may carry stress.
- [*där-* and *här-* adverbs](./dar-har-adverbs/) owns compound adverbs;
  [demonstratives](../pronouns-roles/demonstratives-har-dar/) owns pointing
  phrases such as <Sv>den här boken</Sv>.
- [Connectors](./connectors/) owns the joining word; the sentence-structure
  pages own the clause-order rule.
