<KeyTakeaway>
- Choose a pronoun first by meaning, then by its role in the sentence or noun
  phrase.
- Personal, possessive, reflexive, reciprocal, indefinite, demonstrative, and
  relative forms answer different lookup questions.
- Swedish uses subject and object forms, but this shelf is not a German-style
  noun-case system.
</KeyTakeaway>

Use this section to replace or point to a person, thing, owner, or relation
without mixing the separate pronoun systems.

## Choose by

| If you need to… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| say *I/me, you, he/him, she/her, we/us,* or *they/them* | [Personal pronouns](./personal-pronouns/) |
| say *my, your, his, her, our,* or *their* | [Possessive pronouns](./possessive-pronouns/) |
| point back to a third-person subject's own thing | [<Sv>sin, sitt, sina</Sv>](./sin-sitt-sina/) |
| say *someone, something, some, nobody, nothing,* or *none* | [<Sv>någon, något, ingen, inget</Sv>](./nagon-nagot-ingen-inget/) |
| point at this or that noun | [Demonstratives with <Sv>här</Sv> and <Sv>där</Sv>](./demonstratives-har-dar/) |
| join a relative clause with <Sv>som</Sv>, <Sv>vilket</Sv>, or <Sv>vars</Sv> | [Relative pronouns](./relative-pronouns/) |
| say that the subject acts on itself | [Reflexive pronouns](./reflexive-pronouns/) |
| say *people in general, each other,* or *one each* | [<Sv>man, varandra, var sin</Sv>](./man-varandra-varsin/) |

## Learn in this order

1. [Personal pronouns](./personal-pronouns/) establishes subject and object
   roles.
2. [Possessive pronouns](./possessive-pronouns/) adds owner forms, then
   [<Sv>sin, sitt, sina</Sv>](./sin-sitt-sina/) handles subject-reflexive possession.
3. [<Sv>Någon, något, ingen, inget</Sv>](./nagon-nagot-ingen-inget/) and
   [demonstratives](./demonstratives-har-dar/) expand everyday noun phrases.
4. [Relative pronouns](./relative-pronouns/) connects information across
   clauses.
5. [Reflexive pronouns](./reflexive-pronouns/) and
   [<Sv>man, varandra, var sin</Sv>](./man-varandra-varsin/) complete the main role
   contrasts.

## Watch for

- [Reflexive pronouns](./reflexive-pronouns/) owns <Sv>mig, dig, sig, oss, er</Sv>
  when subject and object refer to the same participant;
  [<Sv>sin, sitt, sina</Sv>](./sin-sitt-sina/) owns reflexive possession.
- [Demonstratives](./demonstratives-har-dar/) points at nouns;
  [Question words](../questions-negation/question-words/) asks for missing
  information; [<Sv>där-</Sv> and <Sv>här-</Sv> adverbs](../prepositions-function-words/dar-har-adverbs/)
  owns compound adverbs such as <Sv>därför</Sv>.
- [<Sv>Varandra</Sv>](./man-varandra-varsin/) is reciprocal—participants act on each
  other—while a reflexive pronoun points back to one clause participant.
