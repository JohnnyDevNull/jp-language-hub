<KeyTakeaway>
- Start with sounds and noun gender, then word order, then verb tenses: each
  block is a prerequisite for the next.
- The grouping is the CEFR level each page already declares, not a new
  judgement about difficulty.
- Cheat sheets and the mistake pages are lookup material for every stage, not
  steps of their own.
</KeyTakeaway>

This is a map, not a lesson. Every entry links to the page that does the
teaching; the note says only why it sits at that point.

## A1 — sounds, nouns, and a first sentence

| # | Page | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
