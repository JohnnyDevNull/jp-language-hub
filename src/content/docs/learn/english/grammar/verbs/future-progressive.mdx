<KeyTakeaway>
- Use the future progressive for an action that will be in progress at a future
  point.
- The form is <span lang="en">will be</span> + <span lang="en">-ing</span>:
  <span lang="en">I will be working at eight.</span>
- It shows the middle of a future action, not the decision to do it.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

The future progressive is the future "middle camera." It places you inside an
action at a future time instead of presenting the action as a whole event.

## Use this now

Choose the future progressive when the sentence answers:
<span lang="en">What will be happening then?</span>

| Future point | Action in progress |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="en">At 8 p.m.</span> | <span lang="en">I will be working.</span> |
| <span lang="en">This time tomorrow</span> | <span lang="en">We will be flying to London.</span> |
| <span lang="en">When you arrive</span> | <span lang="en">They will be waiting outside.</span> |

For a decision, promise or prediction as a whole event, use
[will future](../will-future/). For a plan, use
[going to future](../going-to-future/).

## Core rule

**Pattern:** subject + <span lang="en">will be</span> +
<span lang="en">verb-ing</span>

| Sentence type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| positive | <span lang="en">I will be working.</span> |
| negative | <span lang="en">I will not be working.</span> |
| contraction | <span lang="en">I won't be working.</span> |
| question | <span lang="en">Will you be working?</span> |

The word <span lang="en">be</span> is fixed after
<span lang="en">will</span>. The main verb carries
<span lang="en">-ing</span>.

## Main uses

### A future action in progress

<span lang="en">At nine, I will be driving to the airport.</span>

The driving is already underway at nine.

### A polite question about someone's plans

<span lang="en">Will you be using the car tomorrow?</span>

This often sounds less pushy than
<span lang="en">Will you use the car tomorrow?</span> because it asks about the
plan as background.

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Whole future event" languages={['de', 'en']}>
	<p slot="de">Ich rufe dich um acht an.</p>
	<p slot="en">I will call you at eight.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

<LanguageComparison title="In progress at a future point" languages={['de', 'en']}>
	<p slot="de">Ich arbeite um acht.</p>
	<p slot="en">I will be working at eight.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

German often uses the present tense plus a future time expression where English
may choose a specific future form. The future progressive is useful when the
ongoing action itself matters.

<MistakeBox
	incorrect="At 8, I will be work."
	correct="At 8, I will be working."
	lang="en"
>
	<span lang="en">I will work</span> can be valid when the sentence means a
	decision or a whole future event. If the meaning is "in the middle of work
	at that time," use <span lang="en">will be</span> plus the
	<span lang="en">-ing</span> form.
</MistakeBox>
