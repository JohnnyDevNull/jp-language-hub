English article choice depends on whether a noun is singular or plural,
specific or general, and countable or uncountable. German does not map cleanly
onto these choices.

## Pages

- [Articles](./articles/) explains *a/an*, *the*, and zero article.
- [Countable and uncountable nouns](./countable-and-uncountable-nouns/)
  explains why English says *information*, *advice*, and *equipment* without a
  plural *-s*.
