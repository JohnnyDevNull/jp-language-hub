<KeyTakeaway>
- Most countable nouns add <span lang="en">-s</span>; nouns ending in <span lang="en">-s, -sh, -ch, -x</span>, or <span lang="en">-z</span> usually add <span lang="en">-es</span>.
- A plural noun takes a plural determiner and verb: <span lang="en">these books are</span>.
- Learn common irregular plurals as whole forms.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Plural nouns name more than one countable thing. First check that English
treats the noun as countable; words such as <span lang="en">information</span>
do not simply take a plural <span lang="en">-s</span>.

## Use this now

| Ending or type | Plural | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| most nouns | <span lang="en">-s</span> | <span lang="en">book → books</span> |
| <span lang="en">-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z</span> | <span lang="en">-es</span> | <span lang="en">bus → buses</span> |
| consonant + <span lang="en">-y</span> | <span lang="en">-ies</span> | <span lang="en">city → cities</span> |
| vowel + <span lang="en">-y</span> | <span lang="en">-s</span> | <span lang="en">day → days</span> |

## Core rule

Use plural forms with <span lang="en">these</span>, <span lang="en">those</span>,
<span lang="en">many</span>, and plural verbs: <span lang="en">These new
reports are useful.</span> A singular count noun needs a determiner; a plural
noun can be general with no article: <span lang="en">Reports are useful.</span>

Common irregular forms are <span lang="en">man → men</span>, <span lang="en">woman
→ women</span>, <span lang="en">child → children</span>, <span lang="en">person
→ people</span>, <span lang="en">foot → feet</span>, and <span lang="en">tooth
→ teeth</span>. Treat them as vocabulary, not as a rule to extend.

## Direct comparison

<LanguageComparison title="Plural agreement" languages={['de', 'en']}>
	<p slot="de">Diese Bücher sind neu.</p>
	<p slot="en">These books are new.</p>
</LanguageComparison>

Both languages mark the plural, but English also needs the matching plural
verb <span lang="en">are</span>, not <span lang="en">is</span>.

<MistakeBox incorrect="These book are useful." correct="These books are useful." lang="en">
	A plural determiner such as <span lang="en">these</span> needs a plural noun
	and a plural verb.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Check three things together: number word or determiner, noun form, and verb.
</MemoryTip>
