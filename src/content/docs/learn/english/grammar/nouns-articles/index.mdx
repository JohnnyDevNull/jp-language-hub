- First decide whether the noun is countable and whether the listener can identify it.
- Then choose its article, quantity word, plural, or possessive form.

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

English noun phrases combine several small decisions: countability, general or
specific meaning, number, and ownership. German forms are useful for meaning,
but not a safe pattern for the English choice.

## Choose by

- Need <span lang="en">a/an</span>, <span lang="en">the</span>, or no article? See [articles](./articles/).
- Need a quantity word or need to know whether a noun can be plural? See [countable and uncountable nouns](./countable-and-uncountable-nouns/).
- Need the plural form or its agreement consequence? See [plural nouns](./plural-nouns/).
- Need apostrophe-<span lang="en">s</span> or an <span lang="en">of</span>-phrase? See [possession](./possession/).

## Learn in this order

1. [Countable and uncountable nouns](./countable-and-uncountable-nouns/) for the noun-type decision.
2. [Articles](./articles/) for general, specific, and singular meanings.
3. [Plural nouns](./plural-nouns/) for forms and agreement.
4. [Possession](./possession/) for ownership and relationships.

When a known noun phrase is replaced by a pronoun, continue with
[Pronouns & roles](../pronouns-roles/) to choose the English form for its job
in the clause.
