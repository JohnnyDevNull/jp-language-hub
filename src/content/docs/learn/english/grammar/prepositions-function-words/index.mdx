<KeyTakeaway>
- Choose place or direction first, then choose the preposition that describes that relation.
- Time expressions use different practical questions: point, day, period, start, duration, deadline, or end point.
- Learn dependent prepositions as chunks and connectors by the relationship between two ideas.
</KeyTakeaway>

## Choose by
| Need | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| where something is or where it moves | [Place and direction](./place-and-direction/) |
| when something happens or how long it lasts | [Time prepositions](./time-prepositions/) |
| a verb/adjective plus its usual preposition | [Dependent prepositions](./dependent-prepositions/) |
| addition, contrast, cause, result, or condition | [Connectors](./connectors/) |

## Learn in this order
1. [Place and direction](./place-and-direction/) for everyday movement and location.
2. [Time prepositions](./time-prepositions/) for schedules and durations.
3. [Dependent prepositions](./dependent-prepositions/) as reusable chunks.
4. [Connectors](./connectors/) to join ideas without learning a second word-order system.
