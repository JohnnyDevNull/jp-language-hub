Start with the choice before memorizing the form. English time forms mark more
than time: they also show whether something is routine, temporary, finished,
connected to now, arranged, in progress, or completed before another point.

## Choose by

| Meaning | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| facts, habits, stable situations | [Simple present](./simple-present/) |
| happening now, temporary, changing | [Present progressive](./present-progressive/) |
| choosing between stable and temporary | [Simple present vs present progressive](./simple-present-vs-present-progressive/) |
| finished past time | [Simple past](./simple-past/) |
| action in progress at a past point | [Past progressive](./past-progressive/) |
| earlier than another past point | [Past perfect](./past-perfect/) |
| experience, result, unfinished time, duration up to now | [Present perfect](./present-perfect/) |
| activity continuing to now or showing a present result | [Present perfect progressive](./present-perfect-progressive/) |
| choosing between German *Perfekt* and English past forms | [Simple past vs present perfect](./simple-past-vs-present-perfect/) |
| decisions, promises, offers, predictions | [Will future](./will-future/) |
| plans and visible evidence | [Going to future](./going-to-future/) |
| in progress at a future point | [Future progressive](./future-progressive/) |
| completed before a future point | [Future perfect](./future-perfect/) |

## Learn in this order

1. [Choosing time form](./choosing-time-form/) for the decision path.
2. [Simple present](./simple-present/), [present progressive](./present-progressive/), and their contrast.
3. [Simple past](./simple-past/), [past progressive](./past-progressive/), and [past perfect](./past-perfect/).
4. [Present perfect](./present-perfect/), [present perfect progressive](./present-perfect-progressive/), and [simple past vs present perfect](./simple-past-vs-present-perfect/).
5. [Will future](./will-future/) and [going to future](./going-to-future/).
6. Reference forms: [future progressive](./future-progressive/) and [future perfect](./future-perfect/).
