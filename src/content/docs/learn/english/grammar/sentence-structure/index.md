English sentence structure is stricter than German in a few places that matter
early: statements keep subject before verb, questions move an auxiliary, and
frequency adverbs sit near the verb phrase.

## Pages

- [Sentence order](./sentence-order/) explains the default statement frame and
  why *Yesterday, I met Sarah* does not invert like German.
- [Questions and do-support](./questions-and-do-support/) explains auxiliary
  movement and *do / does / did*.
- [Adverb placement](./adverb-placement/) explains *I often go*, *she is often
  late*, and *I have often wondered*.
