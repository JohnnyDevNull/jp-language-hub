<KeyTakeaway>
- Choose a request form for the relationship and situation, not just a more complicated verb.
- Use conditionals to connect a condition with its realistic or imagined result.
- Reported speech changes a message to a new viewpoint; it owns backshift and reference-word changes.
</KeyTakeaway>

These pages help you make a request, discuss a possible or unreal result, and pass on what someone said.

## Choose by

| If you need to… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| ask for help, permission, or an action | [Polite requests](./polite-requests/) |
| say what happens, may happen, or would have happened | [Conditionals](./conditionals/) |
| tell someone what another person said or asked | [Reported speech](./reported-speech/) |

## Learn in this order

1. [Polite requests](./polite-requests/) gives immediately usable spoken and written patterns.
2. [Conditionals](./conditionals/) builds the main condition-and-result patterns from general facts to unreal pasts.
3. [Reported speech](./reported-speech/) transforms a message from its original viewpoint to yours.
