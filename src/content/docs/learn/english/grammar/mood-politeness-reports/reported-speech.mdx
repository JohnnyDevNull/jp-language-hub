<KeyTakeaway>
- Reported speech gives a message from your viewpoint: *She said that she was tired.*
- After a past reporting verb, backshift is common when the original time is past; it is optional when the information remains true or the context does not need distance.
- Change pronouns and time/place words when the reporter's viewpoint changes: *I → he*, *today → that day*, *here → there*.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Reported speech is not a mechanical tense exercise. First decide what the speaker said, then rebuild it from the new speaker, place, and time.

## Use this now

| Direct words | Reported message |
| --- | --- |
| *“I am tired.”* | *She said (that) she was tired.* |
| *“I have finished.”* | *He said (that) he had finished.* |
| *“Where do you live?”* | *She asked where I lived.* |
| *“Please wait.”* | *She asked me to wait.* |

## Core rule

With a past reporting verb, common backshift moves present to past, past to past perfect, and *will* to *would*. Do not backshift when you report a permanent fact or still-current truth: *The guide said that water boils at 100°C.* Direct wording may also be retained when the time relationship is still clear: *She said she is tired* (for a current report).

### Questions and requests

Reported questions use statement order, not question order:

	caption="Direct and reported question"

Use *if/whether* for a reported yes/no question: *He asked whether I was ready.* For a request, use *asked/told + object + to + verb*: *She told me to wait.*

## Viewpoint words

Change words only if the new viewpoint requires it: *my → her*, *now → then*, *today → that day*, *tomorrow → the next day*, *here → there*. Keep a word when its reference stays the same.

<MistakeBox incorrect="She asked where do I live." correct="She asked where I lived." lang="en">
Once the question is inside a reported clause, use statement order. [Questions and do-support](../../questions-negation/questions-and-do-support/) owns direct-question formation.
</MistakeBox>
