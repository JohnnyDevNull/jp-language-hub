## Learn in this order
1. [Sentence structure](./sentence-structure/) and [Questions & negation](./questions-negation/) establish clause frames.
2. [Nouns & articles](./nouns-articles/) then [Pronouns & roles](./pronouns-roles/) make noun phrases precise.
3. [Verbs & time forms](./verbs-time-forms/) provides the main time decisions; [Verb patterns & voice](./verb-patterns-voice/) adds helpers, modals, voice, and complements.
4. Add [Adjectives & adverbs](./adjectives-adverbs/), [Prepositions & function words](./prepositions-function-words/), [Mood, politeness & reports](./mood-politeness-reports/), and [Word formation & particles](./word-formation-particles/) as your messages grow.
5. Use [Common mistakes](../common-mistakes/) to diagnose persistent transfer instead of memorizing an isolated correction.
