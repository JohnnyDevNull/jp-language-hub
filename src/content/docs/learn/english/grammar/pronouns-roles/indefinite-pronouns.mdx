<KeyTakeaway>
- Use *some-*, *any-*, *no-*, and *every-* compounds for unspecified people,
  things, and places.
- These compounds usually take singular verbs: *Everyone is ready.*
- Use singular *they/them/their* naturally when you refer back to an unknown
  person: *Everyone should bring their ticket.*
</KeyTakeaway>
<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

## Use this now

| Meaning                  | Person     | Thing        | Place        |
| ------------------------ | ---------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| unspecified positive     | _someone_  | _something_  | _somewhere_  |
| question/negative choice | _anyone_   | _anything_   | _anywhere_   |
| zero                     | _no one_   | _nothing_    | _nowhere_    |
| all                      | _everyone_ | _everything_ | _everywhere_ |

## Core rule

Choose the compound for the meaning, then keep its verb singular: _Nobody knows._ Polarity belongs to [Any vs no](../../questions-negation/any-vs-no/): do not make this page a second lesson on double negatives. For noun quantifiers such as _many, much, few,_ and _little_, use [Countable and uncountable nouns](../../nouns-articles/countable-and-uncountable-nouns/).
