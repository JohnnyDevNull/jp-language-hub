<KeyTakeaway>
- Put a possessive determiner before a noun: *my book, their plan*.
- Use an independent possessive form without the noun: *The book is mine; the
  plan is theirs.*
- Use [Possession](../../nouns-articles/possession/) for noun owners such as
  *Maria’s book* and *the roof of the house*.
</KeyTakeaway>
<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

## Use this now

| Before a noun                         | Without the noun                       |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| _my, your, his, her, its, our, their_ | _mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs_ |

## Core rule

The determiner needs a following noun: _our tickets_. The independent form replaces that whole noun phrase: _The tickets are ours._ There is no independent form _its_ in standard modern English.

<MistakeBox incorrect="This book is my." correct="This book is mine." lang="en">
  Use *my* before a noun and *mine* when the noun is omitted.
</MistakeBox>
