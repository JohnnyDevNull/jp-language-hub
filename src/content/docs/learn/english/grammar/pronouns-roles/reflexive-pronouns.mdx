<KeyTakeaway>
- Use a reflexive pronoun when subject and object are the same: *She hurt
  herself.*
- Use it for emphasis after a noun or pronoun: *I wrote it myself.*
- Do not use a reflexive form as a general polite replacement for *me* or *I*.
</KeyTakeaway>
<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

## Core rule

The forms are _myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves,_ and _themselves_. Singular _they_ normally takes _themselves_: _Each person can introduce themselves._ Use a reflexive when the action comes back to its subject, not merely because a pronoun follows a verb.

<MistakeBox
  incorrect="Please contact myself."
  correct="Please contact me."
  lang="en"
>
  *Myself* needs the same subject in the clause, as in *I introduced myself.*
</MistakeBox>
