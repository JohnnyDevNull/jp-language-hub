## Use this now
Check a reliable dictionary for meaning and whether the object can go between the verb and particle. <span lang="en">Turn off the light / Turn the light off / Turn it off.</span>

## Core rule
Some phrasal verbs are separable; others are not: <span lang="en">look after the child</span>, not *look the child after*. Pronunciation can vary by verb and meaning, so check a reliable dictionary with audio rather than applying a general stress rule. Meaning and separability remain the practical first checks.
