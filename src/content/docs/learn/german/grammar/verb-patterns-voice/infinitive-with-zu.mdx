Use zu-infinitives after many verbs, adjectives, nouns, and purpose phrases.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">zu</span> after verbs such as <span lang="de">versuchen</span>, after adjectives such as <span lang="de">wichtig</span>, and in purpose clauses with <span lang="de">um ... zu</span>.

## Core rule

The governor determines whether <span lang="de">zu</span> is needed. Common
patterns are <span lang="de">versuchen zu kommen</span>,
<span lang="de">wichtig zu sein</span>, and purpose
<span lang="de">um zu lernen</span>. With separable verbs,
<span lang="de">zu</span> goes inside the verb:
<span lang="de">anzurufen</span>.
