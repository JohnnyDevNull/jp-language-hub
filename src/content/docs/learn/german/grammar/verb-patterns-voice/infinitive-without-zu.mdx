Use bare infinitives after modal verbs, lassen, gehen, bleiben, sehen, and hören patterns.

## Use this now

Modal verbs and several perception or movement patterns do not use <span lang="de">zu</span>.

## Core rule

Use a bare infinitive after modal verbs (<span lang="de">Ich kann kommen</span>),
after movement verbs for an activity (<span lang="de">Ich gehe einkaufen</span>),
and after perception verbs with an object (<span lang="de">Ich sehe ihn kommen</span>).
<span lang="de">Lassen</span> also takes a bare infinitive:
<span lang="de">Ich lasse ihn arbeiten</span>.
