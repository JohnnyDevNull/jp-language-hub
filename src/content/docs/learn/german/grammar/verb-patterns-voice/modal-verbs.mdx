<KeyTakeaway>
- Modal verbs change the sentence meaning and send the lexical verb to the end.
- Use können, müssen, wollen, sollen, dürfen, mögen with a bare infinitive.
- The modal is finite in the left bracket; the lexical infinitive closes the right bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Use German modal verbs with an infinitive at the right bracket.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">können, müssen, wollen, sollen, dürfen, mögen</span> with a bare infinitive.

## Core rule

The modal is finite in the left bracket; the lexical infinitive waits at the end.
