<KeyTakeaway>
- The nominative names the subject of the clause.
- After sein, werden, and bleiben, a second noun phrase also stays nominative.
- The nominative is the dictionary-like case for article tables.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Use the nominative when a noun phrase does the action or is identified as something.

## Use this now

Choose nominative for <span lang="de">der Mann</span> in <span lang="de">Der Mann liest</span> and for <span lang="de">ein Arzt</span> in <span lang="de">Er ist ein Arzt</span>.

## Core rule

The nominative answers “who or what is this sentence about?” It does not become accusative just because it appears after <span lang="de">sein</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Er ist einen Lehrer."

>
	Identification after <span lang="de">sein</span> uses nominative, not accusative.

<MemoryTip>
Nominative names; it does not receive or suffer the action.
</MemoryTip>
