Use German reflexive pronouns when the subject acts on itself.

## Use this now

Most forms match normal object pronouns; third person uses <span lang="de">sich</span>.

## Core rule

German uses reflexive pronouns in true self-action and in many lexical verbs such as <span lang="de">sich erinnern</span>.

The form depends on person and case: <span lang="de">Ich wasche mich</span>
(accusative), but <span lang="de">Ich wasche mir die Hände</span> (dative
person plus accusative body part). Many verbs must be learned with their
reflexive form, for example <span lang="de">sich erinnern an</span> and
<span lang="de">sich interessieren für</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich wasche mich die Hände."
	correct="Ich wasche mir die Hände."
	lang="de"
>
	With body parts, German often uses dative reflexive for the person and accusative for the body part.
</MistakeBox>
