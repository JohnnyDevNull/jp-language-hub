- Some German verbs take a bare infinitive.
- Modal verbs and several perception or movement patterns do not use zu.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.

Use bare infinitives after modal verbs, lassen, gehen, bleiben, sehen, and hören patterns.

## Use this now

Modal verbs and several perception or movement patterns do not use <span lang="de">zu</span>.

## Core rule

Use <span lang="de">Ich kann kommen</span>, <span lang="de">Ich gehe einkaufen</span>, <span lang="de">Ich sehe ihn kommen</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich kann zu kommen."
	correct="Ich kann kommen."
	lang="de"

</MistakeBox>
