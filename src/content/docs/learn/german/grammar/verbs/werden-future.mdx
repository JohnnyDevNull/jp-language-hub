## Use this now

Use it when the future meaning needs emphasis, prediction, assumption, or formal clarity.

## Core rule

The infinitive goes to the right bracket: <span lang="de">Ich werde morgen anrufen</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich werde morgen anrufe."
	correct="Ich werde morgen anrufen."
	lang="de"
>
	The lexical verb after <span lang="de">werden</span> is an infinitive.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Werden-future adds stance; present plus time often adds enough time.
</MemoryTip>
