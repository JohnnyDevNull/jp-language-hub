Use würde plus infinitive for productive Konjunktiv II forms.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">würde</span> plus infinitive when the one-word Konjunktiv II form is rare, awkward, or too literary.

## Core rule

Do not overuse it for <span lang="de">sein</span>, <span lang="de">haben</span>, and common modals, where one-word forms are very common.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich würde Zeit haben, wenn ich hätte frei."
	correct="Ich hätte Zeit, wenn ich frei hätte."
	lang="de"
>
	Use common one-word forms where they are natural.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Würde is the fallback, not the only tool.
</MemoryTip>
