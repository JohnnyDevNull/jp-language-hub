<KeyTakeaway>
- Inseparable prefixes stay attached in all forms.
- Common inseparable prefixes are usually unstressed and do not take ge- in the participle.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Keep be-, emp-, ent-, er-, ge-, miss-, ver-, and zer- attached to the verb.

## Use this now

Common inseparable prefixes are usually unstressed and do not take ge- in the participle.

## Core rule

<span lang="de">besuchen</span> becomes <span lang="de">besucht</span>, not <span lang="de">gebesucht</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich suche dich be."
	correct="Ich besuche dich."
	lang="de"
>
	Inseparable prefixes do not move to the right bracket.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
If the prefix is unstressed and inseparable, keep it glued to the verb.
</MemoryTip>
