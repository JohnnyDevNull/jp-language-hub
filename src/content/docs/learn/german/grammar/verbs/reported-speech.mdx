Use reported speech to mark that information comes from someone else.

## Use this now

In careful writing, German often uses Konjunktiv I for reported statements. In everyday speech, indicative or Konjunktiv II may appear.

## Core rule

The main task is source marking: <span lang="de">Er sagt, er habe keine Zeit</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Er sagt, er hat keine Zeit."
	correct="Er sagt, er habe keine Zeit."
	lang="de"
>
	In formal reported speech, Konjunktiv I marks the statement as reported.
</MistakeBox>
