Give German commands with du, ihr, Sie, and wir forms.

## Use this now

Use the verb stem for many <span lang="de">du</span> commands, the normal <span lang="de">ihr</span> form for plural informal commands, and verb-first with <span lang="de">Sie</span> for polite commands.

## Core rule

Command forms often drop the subject except for <span lang="de">Sie</span> and <span lang="de">wir</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Du komm hier."
	correct="Komm hier."
	lang="de"
>
	A normal <span lang="de">du</span> imperative drops the subject.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Commands are verb-first and audience-sensitive.
</MemoryTip>
