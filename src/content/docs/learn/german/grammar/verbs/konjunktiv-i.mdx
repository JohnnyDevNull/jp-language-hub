Recognize Konjunktiv I in formal reported speech.

## Use this now

Look for forms such as <span lang="de">sei</span>, <span lang="de">habe</span>, <span lang="de">gehe</span>, and plural forms that may switch to Konjunktiv II when ambiguous.

## Core rule

You do not need to use it constantly in conversation, but you need to recognize it in news and formal texts.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Der Minister sagt, er ist zufrieden."
	correct="Der Minister sagt, er sei zufrieden."
	lang="de"
>
	Formal reported speech often uses Konjunktiv I.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Konjunktiv I puts quotation marks into the grammar.
</MemoryTip>
