<KeyTakeaway>
- Separable prefixes split in finite main clauses.
- Use the finite verb in the left bracket and the particle in the right bracket: Ich rufe dich an.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Place separable prefixes at the right bracket in main clauses.

## Use this now

Use the finite verb in the left bracket and the particle in the right bracket: <span lang="de">Ich rufe dich an</span>.

## Core rule

In infinitives, participles, and subordinate clauses, the prefix usually stays attached.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich anrufe dich."
	correct="Ich rufe dich an."
	lang="de"
>
	The finite verb goes second; the particle goes to the right bracket.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Separable means “two positions in main clauses,” not two meanings.
</MemoryTip>
