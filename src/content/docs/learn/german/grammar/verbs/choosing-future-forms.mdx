Choose between present-for-future, werden-future, and future perfect.

## Use this now

Use present for concrete plans, werden-future for prediction or emphasis, and future perfect for completion before another future point.

## Core rule

If a future time word already makes the time clear, do not add <span lang="de">werden</span> unless it adds stance.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich werde morgen ins Kino gehe."
	correct="Ich gehe morgen ins Kino."
	lang="de"
>
	The simple planned future normally uses present tense, not a malformed werden form.
</MistakeBox>
