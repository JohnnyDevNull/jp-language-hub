<KeyTakeaway>
- Preterite is normal in writing and with sein, haben, werden, and modal verbs.
- In speech, many lexical verbs prefer perfect, but war, hatte, and modal preterites are common.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

Use German preterite for written narrative and common high-frequency verbs.

## Use this now

In speech, many lexical verbs prefer perfect, but <span lang="de">war</span>, <span lang="de">hatte</span>, and modal preterites are common.

## Core rule

Use preterite to narrate compactly: <span lang="de">Er ging nach Hause</span>.
