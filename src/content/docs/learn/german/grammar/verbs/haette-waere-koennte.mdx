Prioritize the high-frequency Konjunktiv II forms hätte, wäre, and könnte.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">hätte</span> for would have, <span lang="de">wäre</span> for would be, and <span lang="de">könnte</span> for could.

## Core rule

They make polite and hypothetical German sound natural without a heavy würde chain.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich würde sein froh."
	correct="Ich wäre froh."
	lang="de"
>
	<span lang="de">Wäre</span> is the natural Konjunktiv II form of <span lang="de">sein</span>.
</MistakeBox>
