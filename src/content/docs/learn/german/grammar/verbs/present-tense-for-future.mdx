Use German present with a future time expression for plans and schedules.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">morgen</span>, <span lang="de">nächste Woche</span>, or another future cue to make the time clear.

## Core rule

This is normal, not lazy: <span lang="de">Morgen fahre ich nach Hamburg</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Morgen werde ich fahre."
	correct="Morgen fahre ich."
	lang="de"
>
	For a simple plan, present tense with a future time word is enough.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
German often lets the time word do the future work.
</MemoryTip>
