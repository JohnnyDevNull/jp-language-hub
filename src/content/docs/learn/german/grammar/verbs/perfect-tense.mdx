<KeyTakeaway>
- The perfect is the everyday past in conversation.
- Most verbs use haben; many movement and change-of-state verbs use sein.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Use haben or sein plus participle for the normal spoken German past.

## Use this now

Most verbs use <span lang="de">haben</span>; many movement and change-of-state verbs use <span lang="de">sein</span>.

## Core rule

The participle closes the verb bracket: <span lang="de">Ich habe das Buch gelesen</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich habe nach Berlin gegangen."
	correct="Ich bin nach Berlin gegangen."
	lang="de"
>
	Directional movement with <span lang="de">gehen</span> uses <span lang="de">sein</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Spoken past is usually auxiliary plus participle.
</MemoryTip>
