Use active voice when the actor is the natural sentence focus.

## Use this now

Use active when the actor is known, important, or responsible.

## Core rule

The subject performs the action: <span lang="de">Die Firma baut das Haus</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Das Haus baut von der Firma."
	correct="Die Firma baut das Haus."
	lang="de"
>
	If the company is the focus and actor, active voice is the natural choice.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Active answers “who does it?” first.
</MemoryTip>
