<KeyTakeaway>
- Each modal carries a different speaker attitude.
- Choose können for ability, müssen for necessity, wollen for intention, sollen for advice/obligation, and dürfen for permission.
- German verb forms become easier when you place them inside the verb bracket.
</KeyTakeaway>

Choose the German modal from ability, necessity, wish, advice, permission, or preference.

## Use this now

Choose <span lang="de">können</span> for ability, <span lang="de">müssen</span> for necessity, <span lang="de">wollen</span> for intention, <span lang="de">sollen</span> for advice/obligation, and <span lang="de">dürfen</span> for permission.

## Core rule

Do not translate English “must”, “should”, and “may” one-to-one without checking meaning.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich muss vielleicht kommen."
	correct="Ich sollte vielleicht kommen."
	lang="de"
>
	For mild advice or recommendation, <span lang="de">sollte</span> is often better than <span lang="de">muss</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Modals are meanings, not just verb forms.
</MemoryTip>
