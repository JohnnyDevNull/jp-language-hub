Choose among German present, perfect, and preterite for real communication.

## Use this now

Use present for now/future plans, perfect for everyday spoken past, and preterite for narratives or common verbs.

## Core rule

Do not copy English present perfect choices into German automatically.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich habe hier seit 2020 gewohnt."
	correct="Ich wohne hier seit 2020."
	lang="de"
>
	German uses present for situations that began in the past and continue now.
</MistakeBox>
