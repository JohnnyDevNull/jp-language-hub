Keep be-, emp-, ent-, er-, ge-, miss-, ver-, and zer- attached to the verb.

## Use this now

Common inseparable prefixes are usually unstressed and do not take ge- in the participle.

## Core rule

Inseparable prefixes stay attached in main clauses, infinitives, and participles.

| Infinitive | Main clause | Participle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="de">besuchen</span> | <span lang="de">Ich besuche sie.</span> | <span lang="de">besucht</span> |
| <span lang="de">verstehen</span> | <span lang="de">Ich verstehe das.</span> | <span lang="de">verstanden</span> |



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich suche dich be."
	correct="Ich besuche dich."
	lang="de"
>
	Inseparable prefixes do not move to the right bracket.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
If the prefix is unstressed and inseparable, keep it glued to the verb.
</MemoryTip>
