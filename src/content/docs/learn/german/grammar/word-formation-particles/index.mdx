German prefixes can change a verb's meaning and, for separable verbs, place a
piece of it at the end of a main clause. Keep meaning and clause form as two
separate lookup questions.

## Learn in this order

1. [Particle verbs](./particle-verbs/) gives the whole-unit idea.
2. [Separable verbs](./separable-verbs/) gives the main-clause, infinitive, and
   participle patterns.
3. [Inseparable prefix verbs](./inseparable-prefix-verbs/) gives the contrasting pattern.
4. Use [Particle verbs vs plain verbs](./particle-verbs-vs-plain-verbs/) for
   difficult meaning choices such as <span lang="de">anfangen</span> versus
   <span lang="de">warten auf</span>.
5. Use [Compound nouns](./compound-nouns/) to find the final noun, its gender,
   and the productive compound form.
