Adjectives and adverbs are easier after cases and articles. Attributive
adjectives share the noun-phrase signal; predicate adjectives and adverbs do
not take the same endings.

## The signal model

Use [adjective endings](./adjective-endings/) only after the noun phrase has a
case and article context. German wants the noun phrase to show gender, number,
and case somewhere. If the article shows the signal, the adjective can often
use a lighter ending. If the article is missing or ambiguous, the adjective
does more work.

## Description vs manner

- Before a noun: adjective endings matter, as in *ein guter Kaffee*.
- After *sein*, *werden*, or *bleiben*: no adjective ending, as in *Der Kaffee
  ist gut*.
- Describing an action: German often keeps the same basic form, as in *Sie
  spricht ruhig*.
