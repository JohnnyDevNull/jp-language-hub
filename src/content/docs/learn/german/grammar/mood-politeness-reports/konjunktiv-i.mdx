Recognize Konjunktiv I in formal reported speech.

## Use this now

Look for forms such as <span lang="de">sei</span>, <span lang="de">habe</span>, <span lang="de">gehe</span>, and plural forms that may switch to Konjunktiv II when ambiguous.

## Core rule

You do not need it constantly in conversation, but you need to recognize it in
news and formal texts. Formal reporting can keep the source at a distance:

| Direct statement | Formal report |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="de">Er ist zufrieden.</span> | <span lang="de">Er sei zufrieden.</span> |
| <span lang="de">Sie hat Zeit.</span> | <span lang="de">Sie habe Zeit.</span> |

If a Konjunktiv I form looks identical to the indicative, formal German may use
Konjunktiv II instead to make the distance visible.
