Use würde plus infinitive for productive Konjunktiv II forms.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">würde</span> plus infinitive when the one-word Konjunktiv II form is rare, awkward, or too literary.

## Core rule

Use <span lang="de">würde</span> as the finite verb and the lexical infinitive
in the right bracket: <span lang="de">Ich würde morgen arbeiten</span>. Do not
overuse it for <span lang="de">sein</span>, <span lang="de">haben</span>, and
common modals, where one-word forms are normally better.



<MistakeBox
		incorrect="Ich würde sein zu Hause."
		correct="Ich wäre zu Hause."
	lang="de"
>
		For <span lang="de">sein</span>, German normally uses the one-word form <span lang="de">wäre</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Würde is the fallback, not the only tool.
</MemoryTip>
