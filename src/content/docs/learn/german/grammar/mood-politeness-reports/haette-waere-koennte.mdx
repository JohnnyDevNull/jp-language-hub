Prioritize the high-frequency Konjunktiv II forms hätte, wäre, and könnte.

## Use this now

Choose the form by the ordinary verb or meaning: possession/availability,
state, or possibility.

## Core rule

| Need | Ready-made form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| have or have time | <span lang="de">hätte</span> | <span lang="de">Ich hätte morgen Zeit.</span> |
| be in a state | <span lang="de">wäre</span> | <span lang="de">Das wäre hilfreich.</span> |
| be able to / could | <span lang="de">könnte</span> | <span lang="de">Könntest du helfen?</span> |

These forms make hypothetical and polite German natural without a long
<span lang="de">würde</span> construction.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich würde sein froh."
	correct="Ich wäre froh."
	lang="de"
>
	<span lang="de">Wäre</span> is the natural Konjunktiv II form of <span lang="de">sein</span>.
</MistakeBox>
