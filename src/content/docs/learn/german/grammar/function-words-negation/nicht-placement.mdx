## Use this now

Put <span lang="de">nicht</span> before what it specifically negates; for whole-clause negation it often comes late but before final verb parts.

## Core rule

In a verb bracket, <span lang="de">nicht</span> usually stands before the right bracket: <span lang="de">Ich kann heute nicht kommen</span>.

When <span lang="de">nicht</span> negates a particular phrase, place it directly
before that phrase: <span lang="de">Nicht heute, sondern morgen</span>.
For broad clause negation, place it late in the middle field, before a final
participle, infinitive, or separable particle.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich kann heute kommen nicht."
	correct="Ich kann heute nicht kommen."
	lang="de"
>
	The infinitive forms the right bracket, and <span lang="de">nicht</span> stands before it.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Nicht usually stands just before the thing it cancels.
</MemoryTip>
