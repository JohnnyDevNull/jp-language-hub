Choose German negation from what you are negating: noun phrase, verb, phrase, or clause.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">kein</span> for a negated indefinite noun phrase and <span lang="de">nicht</span> for most other negation.

## Core rule

Negation is about scope: decide exactly what is denied, then choose and place the negative word.

| What is denied? | Typical form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| an indefinite noun phrase | <span lang="de">kein</span> | <span lang="de">Ich habe kein Auto.</span> |
| an adjective, adverb, or verb phrase | <span lang="de">nicht</span> | <span lang="de">Das ist nicht teuer.</span> |
| a person, thing, time, or place | negative pronoun/adverb | <span lang="de">Niemand kommt.</span> |

Use the focused pages for the form choice and for the exact position of
<span lang="de">nicht</span>; this overview keeps the decision layers separate.
