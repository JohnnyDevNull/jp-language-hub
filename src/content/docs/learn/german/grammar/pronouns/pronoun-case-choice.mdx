Choose German pronoun case from the same role logic as nouns.

## Use this now

Use the verb, preposition, or sentence role to choose nominative, accusative, or dative.

## Core rule

The case question is the same for nouns and pronouns: subject, direct object, recipient, fixed preposition, or location/direction.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich gebe er das Buch."
	correct="Ich gebe ihm das Buch."
	lang="de"
>
	The receiver is dative, so <span lang="de">er</span> becomes <span lang="de">ihm</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Replace the noun phrase only after you know its case.
