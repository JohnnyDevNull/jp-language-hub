Use German personal pronouns in nominative, accusative, and dative forms.

## Use this now

Use nominative for subjects, accusative for direct objects, and dative for recipients or dative prepositions.

## Core rule

Personal pronouns replace noun phrases and keep the role of the phrase they replace.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich sehe er."
	correct="Ich sehe ihn."
	lang="de"
>
	A direct-object masculine pronoun is accusative <span lang="de">ihn</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Subject forms answer who does it; object forms answer who receives or is affected.
