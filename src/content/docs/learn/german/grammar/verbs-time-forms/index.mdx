<KeyTakeaway>
- Choose time form from the event's time perspective and register.
- German present covers many current actions and concrete future plans.
- Perfect is the everyday spoken past; preterite remains central in writing and with high-frequency verbs.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

German time-form choices are practical decisions, not a list to memorize.
Start with the time perspective and whether the event is complete, then choose
the form that fits speech, writing, or future emphasis.

## Choose by situation

| Meaning or problem | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| choosing the right present, past, or future form | [Choosing time form](./choosing-time-form/) |
| now, habits, general truths, or a near future | [Present tense](./present-tense/) |
| an ordinary completed event in conversation | [Perfect tense](./perfect-tense/) |
| written narrative or common forms of <span lang="de">sein</span>, <span lang="de">haben</span>, and modals | [Preterite](./preterite/) |
| choosing between present, perfect, and preterite | [Present vs perfect vs preterite](./present-vs-perfect-vs-preterite/) |
| prediction, assumption, or explicit future emphasis | [Werden future](./werden-future/) |
| a scheduled future event | [Present tense for future](./present-tense-for-future/) |
| comparing future options | [Choosing future forms](./choosing-future-forms/) |
| completion before a future point or an assumption about completion | [Future perfect](./future-perfect/) |
| an event completed before another past event | [Past perfect](./past-perfect/) |

## Learn in this order

1. Ask whether the event is present, completed, ongoing in the past, or
   located before another reference point.
2. Use [Choosing time form](./choosing-time-form/) when several forms could
   fit.
3. Use the detail page for formation and high-frequency examples.
4. Keep the verb bracket in view: participles and infinitives often occupy the
   right edge of the clause.

Reference tail: [Future perfect](./future-perfect/) remains available for
completion before a future point or past assumptions, outside the core
beginner route.
