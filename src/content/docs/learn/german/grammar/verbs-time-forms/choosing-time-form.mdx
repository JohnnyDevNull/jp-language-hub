Choose German present, perfect, preterite, future, or future perfect from meaning and register.

## Use this now

Use present for now, habits, and scheduled future; perfect for most spoken past; preterite for narratives and common verbs; werden-future for prediction or emphasis; future perfect for completion before a future point.

## Core rule

Choose the speaker task first, then use the smallest form that answers it:

| Task | Default form | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| now, habit, or scheduled future | present | <span lang="de">Ich arbeite heute. Morgen fahre ich.</span> |
| completed event in conversation | perfect | <span lang="de">Ich habe den Film gesehen.</span> |
| written narrative or <span lang="de">sein</span>/<span lang="de">haben</span>/modal | preterite | <span lang="de">Er war müde und ging nach Hause.</span> |
| prediction or explicit future stance | <span lang="de">werden</span> + infinitive | <span lang="de">Es wird regnen.</span> |
| completed before a future point | future perfect | <span lang="de">Bis Freitag werde ich es erledigt haben.</span> |
