## Use this now

Use it when the future meaning needs emphasis, prediction, assumption, or formal clarity.

## Core rule

Conjugate <span lang="de">werden</span> and place the lexical infinitive in
the right bracket: <span lang="de">Ich werde morgen anrufen</span>. The same
form can express a prediction (<span lang="de">Es wird regnen</span>), a
promise (<span lang="de">Ich werde helfen</span>), or an assumption about now
(<span lang="de">Sie wird zu Hause sein</span>).
