Use haben or sein plus participle for the normal spoken German past.

## Use this now

Most verbs use <span lang="de">haben</span>; many movement and change-of-state verbs use <span lang="de">sein</span>.

## Core rule

Form the perfect with present-tense <span lang="de">haben</span> or
<span lang="de">sein</span> plus a past participle. Use
<span lang="de">sein</span> mainly with movement/change of state and
<span lang="de">haben</span> for most other verbs. The participle closes the
verb bracket: <span lang="de">Ich habe das Buch gelesen</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich habe nach Berlin gegangen."
	correct="Ich bin nach Berlin gegangen."
	lang="de"
>
	Directional movement with <span lang="de">gehen</span> uses <span lang="de">sein</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>

</MemoryTip>
