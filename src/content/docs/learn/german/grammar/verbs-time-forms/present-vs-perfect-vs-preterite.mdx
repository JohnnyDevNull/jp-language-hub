Choose among German present, perfect, and preterite for real communication.

## Use this now

Use present for now/future plans, perfect for everyday spoken past, and preterite for narratives or common verbs.

## Core rule

Do not copy English present-perfect choices into German automatically. Ask
whether the situation is still true, whether the past is conversational, or
whether the text is a narrative:

| Context | German default | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| began before now and continues | present | <span lang="de">Ich wohne seit 2020 hier.</span> |
| completed event in conversation | perfect | <span lang="de">Ich habe ihn gestern gesehen.</span> |
| written/story past or common auxiliary/modal | preterite | <span lang="de">Es war einmal.</span> |
