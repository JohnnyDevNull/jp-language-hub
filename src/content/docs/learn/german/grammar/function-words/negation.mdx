Choose German negation from what you are negating: noun phrase, verb, phrase, or clause.

## Use this now

Use <span lang="de">kein</span> for a negated indefinite noun phrase and <span lang="de">nicht</span> for most other negation.

## Core rule

Negation is about scope: decide exactly what is denied, then choose and place the negative word.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="Ich habe nicht Auto."
	correct="Ich habe kein Auto."
	lang="de"
>
	A negated indefinite noun phrase takes <span lang="de">kein</span>.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Negation asks two questions: what is denied, and where does that denial attach?
