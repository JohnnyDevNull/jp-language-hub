Adjectives and adverbs are easier after cases and articles. Attributive
adjectives share the noun-phrase signal; predicate adjectives and adverbs do
not take the same endings.

## The signal model

German wants the noun phrase to show gender, number, and case somewhere. If the
article shows the signal, the adjective can often use a weaker ending. If the
article is missing or ambiguous, the adjective does more work.

## Description vs manner

- Before a noun: <span lang="de">ein guter Kaffee</span> needs an adjective ending.
- After <span lang="de">sein</span>, <span lang="de">werden</span>, or
  <span lang="de">bleiben</span>: <span lang="de">Der Kaffee ist gut</span> has no
  attributive ending.
- Describing an action: <span lang="de">Sie spricht ruhig</span> uses an
  uninflected adverb.
