<KeyTakeaway>
- In a main clause, the finite verb opens the bracket and a non-finite verb part closes it.
- Participles, infinitives, and separable particles can form the right bracket.
- The middle field holds objects, time, and other information between the verb parts.
</KeyTakeaway>

Use the German verb bracket to place infinitives, participles, particles, and negation.

## Use this now

Identify the finite verb, then look for its participle, infinitive, or separable
particle. Put that second verb part at the end of the main clause.

## Core rule

German main clauses often split a multi-part verb: the finite verb comes in
second position and the non-finite part forms the right bracket.
