<KeyTakeaway>
- A subordinating conjunction sends the finite verb to the end of its clause.
- The subject and other clause material stay before that final verb.
- In a sentence with a main clause, the subordinate clause can come first or second.
</KeyTakeaway>

Move the finite verb to the end in German subordinate clauses.

## Use this now

Find the subordinating conjunction, then move the finite verb to the end of
that clause. If the subordinate clause comes first, the main clause still
starts with its finite verb after the comma.

## Core rule

In a subordinate clause introduced by words such as `weil`, `dass`, or `wenn`,
the finite verb occupies the final position. A following main clause keeps its
own V2 order.
