Use German relative pronouns with case and verb-final order.

## Use this now

Start with the noun's gender and number. Then ask what role the relative
pronoun has in its own clause to choose nominative, accusative, or dative.

## Core rule

Relative clauses are subordinate clauses: the relative pronoun starts the
clause and the finite verb comes last. The pronoun's case is independent of
the noun's role in the main clause.
