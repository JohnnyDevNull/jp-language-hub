<KeyTakeaway>
- Many German infinitive clauses use `zu` before the infinitive.
- The infinitive stays at the end of its clause.
- Separable verbs place `zu` between the prefix and the verb stem: `aufzustehen`.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Use zu-infinitive clauses after many German verbs, nouns, and adjectives.

## Use this now

Check whether the governing expression licenses a `zu`-infinitive. Put `zu`
before the infinitive, or inside a separable verb, and place the infinitive
clause at the end.

## Core rule

The governing clause contains the finite verb; the dependent infinitive clause
contains `zu` plus an infinitive. The infinitive is final, while a separable
prefix surrounds `zu`.
