Build German yes/no questions and question-word questions with verb position.

## Use this now

Find the finite verb first. Then identify whether the clause is a statement, a question, or a subordinate clause.

## Core rule

German word order is field-based: the finite verb anchors the clause, and other sentence parts arrange around it.
