- German clause shape depends on the finite verb position.
- Main clauses, questions, and subordinate clauses each have a reliable pattern.
- The verb bracket explains why important verb parts often appear late.

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Use weil, dass, wenn, obwohl, and similar words to send the finite verb to the end.

## Use this now

Find the finite verb first. Then identify whether the clause is a statement, a question, or a subordinate clause.

## Core rule

German word order is field-based: the finite verb anchors the clause, and other sentence parts arrange around it.
