- `weil`, `dass`, `wenn`, and `obwohl` introduce subordinate clauses.

- Choose the conjunction for meaning; the clause-order effect is shared.

Use weil, dass, wenn, obwohl, and similar words to send the finite verb to the end.

## Use this now

Choose the conjunction by meaning first. Then put the finite verb at the end of
the clause it introduces.

## Core rule

Subordinating conjunctions connect a dependent clause to another clause and
make that dependent clause verb-final. The conjunction itself remains at the
start of its clause.
