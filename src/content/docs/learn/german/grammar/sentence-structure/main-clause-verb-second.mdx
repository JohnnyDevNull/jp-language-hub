- In a German declarative main clause, the finite verb is in position two.
- The first position can be one element or phrase; the subject does not have to come first.
- Move the finite verb with the first field, not after the subject.

Put the finite verb in second position in German declarative main clauses.

## Use this now

Find the finite verb and count fields, not individual words. Put exactly one
constituent before it in a declarative main clause.

## Core rule

German main clauses use verb-second (V2) order. The first field may be the
subject, a time phrase, or an object; the finite verb follows it, and the
remaining material comes after the verb.
