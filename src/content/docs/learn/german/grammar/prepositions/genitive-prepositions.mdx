<KeyTakeaway>
- Trotz, während, wegen, and statt often take genitive in standard writing.
- Dative variants are common in speech for some prepositions.
- Reading formal German is easier when you recognize the genitive signal.
</KeyTakeaway>

<TagList levels={frontmatter.levels} tags={frontmatter.tags} />

Genitive prepositions are less urgent for first speaking, but important for reading, writing, and formal style.

## Use this now

Expect genitive in careful writing after <span lang="de">trotz</span>, <span lang="de">während</span>, <span lang="de">wegen</span>, and <span lang="de">statt</span>.

## Core rule

A genitive preposition assigns genitive to the following noun phrase: <span lang="de">wegen des Regens</span>, <span lang="de">während der Woche</span>.



<MistakeBox
	incorrect="während dem Meeting"
	correct="während des Meetings"
	lang="de"
>
	In standard written German, <span lang="de">während</span> takes genitive.
</MistakeBox>

<MemoryTip>
Genitive prepositions often sound compact and formal: despite, during, because of, instead of.
</MemoryTip>
