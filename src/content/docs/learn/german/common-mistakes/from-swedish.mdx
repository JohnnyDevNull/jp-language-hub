Fix common German mistakes caused by Swedish word order, definiteness, tense, and particle habits.

## Use this now

Use Swedish as a V2 bridge, then re-check German case, article gender, separable-prefix placement, and subordinate verb-final order.

## Core rule

Swedish and German share some Germanic structure, but German exposes more morphology in articles, pronouns, cases, and adjective endings.

## Diagnostic map

| Swedish habit | German repair | Go to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| trusting V2 everywhere | keep V2 in main clauses, but send finite verbs late in subordinate clauses | [Subordinate clauses](../../grammar/sentence-structure/subordinate-clauses/) |
| using definiteness endings as the main noun signal | learn German article plus gender with the noun | [Articles and gender](../../grammar/nouns-articles/articles-and-gender/) |
| treating Swedish common/neuter as enough | choose German masculine, feminine, neuter, or plural signals | [Der, die, das](../../grammar/nouns-articles/der-die-das/) |
| mapping particles by feel | learn German particle verbs as whole meaning units | [Separable verbs](../../grammar/verbs/separable-verbs/) |
| letting Swedish hide object roles | decide nominative, accusative, or dative before choosing forms | [Cases as roles](../../grammar/cases/cases-as-roles/) |
| skipping adjective agreement after the article | share the signal between article and adjective | [Adjective endings](../../grammar/adjectives-adverbs/adjective-endings/) |
