Fix common German mistakes caused by English tense, word order, article, and preposition habits.

## Use this now

Check for these English exports first: progressive forms, no case on articles, fixed subject-verb order, and one-to-one preposition translation.

## Core rule

English hides many grammatical roles that German marks overtly. When German sounds wrong, ask whether English made you ignore case, verb position, or article signals.

## Diagnostic map

| English habit | German repair | Go to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| progressive for ordinary present | use German present tense unless you need another construction | [Present tense](../../grammar/verbs-time-forms/present-tense/) |
| subject-verb order after a fronted phrase | keep the finite verb in position two | [Main clause verb second](../../grammar/sentence-structure/main-clause-verb-second/) |
| no visible case on articles | choose the sentence role, then the article signal | [Cases as roles](../../grammar/cases/cases-as-roles/) |
| prepositions translated word for word | learn the German preposition plus its case | [Prepositions and case](../../grammar/prepositions/prepositions-and-case/) |
| "would" for every conditional or polite form | choose between <span lang="de">würde</span> and one-word Konjunktiv forms | [Konjunktiv II](../../grammar/mood-politeness-reports/konjunktiv-ii/) |
| passive copied mechanically | choose active, passive, or <span lang="de">man</span> by focus | [Active vs passive](../../grammar/verb-patterns-voice/active-vs-passive/) |
