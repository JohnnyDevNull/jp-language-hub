Use these pages when the sentence feels plausible because English or Swedish
made it plausible. The goal is diagnosis: spot the source-language habit, then
open the German rule that fixes that exact pattern.

## Choose by source language

| If your sentence is influenced by... | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| English tense, passive, articles, prepositions, modal meanings, or stable subject-verb order | [English to German mistakes](./from-english/) |
| Swedish V2, definiteness, particles, adjective agreement, or similar-looking Germanic patterns | [Swedish to German mistakes](./from-swedish/) |

## What the mistake hubs do

- They show the likely learner sentence or decision.
- They explain why it follows English or Swedish logic.
- They send you to the German page that repairs the pattern.
