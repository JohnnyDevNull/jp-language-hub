---
title: Swedish sin, sitt, and sina — quick guide
description: A short guide to Swedish third-person reflexive possessives.
---

Use <span lang="sv">sin</span>, <span lang="sv">sitt</span>, or
<span lang="sv">sina</span> when a third-person subject possesses something
and the possessive refers back to that same subject:

- <span lang="sv">Anna tar sin jacka.</span> — her own en noun
- <span lang="sv">Anna säljer sitt hus.</span> — her own ett noun
- <span lang="sv">Anna träffar sina vänner.</span> — her own plural noun

The form agrees with the possessed noun, not with the possessor. Use
<span lang="sv">hans</span>, <span lang="sv">hennes</span>, or
<span lang="sv">deras</span> when referring to somebody else.

For clause boundaries, contrasts, and common mistakes, read the canonical
[sin, sitt, and sina reference](../../../grammar/pronouns-function-words/sin-sitt-sina/).
