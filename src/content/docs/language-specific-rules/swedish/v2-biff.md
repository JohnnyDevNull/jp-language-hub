---
title: Swedish V2 and BIFF — quick guide
description: A short guide to finite-verb and inte placement in Swedish clauses.
---

V2 and BIFF are complementary learning rules for Swedish word order:

- **V2:** In a main clause, the finite verb normally occupies the second
  sentence position: <span lang="sv">Idag köper jag en bok.</span>
- **BIFF:** In a subordinate clause, <span lang="sv">inte</span> normally comes
  before the finite verb: <span lang="sv">… eftersom jag inte köper boken.</span>

The “second position” is the second constituent, not necessarily the second
individual word. BIFF is a memory aid, not a complete account of every
subordinate-clause rule.
