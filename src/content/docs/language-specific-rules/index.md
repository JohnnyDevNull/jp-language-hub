This section summarizes language-specific behavior and links to the canonical
grammar explanation. It is a quick-reference layer, not a second source of
truth.

## German

Planned topics include cases, grammatical gender, article and adjective
declension, separable verbs, verb-final subordinate clauses, and two-way
prepositions.

## English

Planned topics include simple and progressive forms, do-support, article usage,
countable and uncountable nouns, phrasal verbs, and irregular verbs.

## Swedish

Swedish is the current learning focus. The first references will cover:

- en and ett nouns
- indefinite, definite, and plural noun forms
- adjective agreement and comparison
- V2, inversion, subordinate-clause word order, and BIFF
- verb groups, the supine, and particle verbs
- sin, sitt, and sina
- common pronoun and preposition patterns
